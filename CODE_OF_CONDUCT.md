# Code of Conduct

Boxwarden is a technical project. This document is short because the rule is short.

## Scope

Project spaces — including issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages, and
other Boxwarden discussion — are for the project: its design, code, behavior,
documentation, security, operation, and contribution process.

Unrelated political, ideological, religious, and personal disputes are off topic,
whether or not you believe your position is correct, and whether or not someone
else raised the subject first.

## Conduct

- Criticize the work as hard as you like. Do not attack the person.
- Do not use anyone's identity, politics, beliefs, affiliations, or personal
  characteristics as an argument, an insult, or a credential.
- No harassment, threats, sustained disruption, or deliberate bad faith.

Reporting a specific incident to the maintainers is not itself off topic.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, close, or delete content, and may restrict or
block accounts, at their discretion.

This is not a court or employment process. Maintainers may consider whatever
context and evidence they find useful, but there is no formal hearing or appeal
process, and maintainers are not obligated to publish or justify moderation
decisions.

Nothing here entitles anyone to a response, continued participation, or any
particular outcome.

Off-topic content may be removed without any finding that anyone did anything
wrong.
